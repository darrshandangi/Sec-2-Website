Longevity & Performance

Care & Quick Setup Guide

Proper maintenance ensures that FoldBeat retains its acoustic resonance and pristine architectural finish for years.

🧼

Chassis Care

Wipe the composite satin panels with a clean, dry microfiber cloth. Avoid alcohol, harsh acetone, or abrasive cleaning chemicals.

💧

IPX4 Water Limits

FoldBeat withstands desk beverage spills, kitchen splashes, and light rain. It is not designed for total water submersion or pool diving.

⚡

Charging Specifications

Use a standard 5V/2A or 9V/2A USB-C power adapter. LED Meanings: Solid Amber = Charging, Solid White = Fully Charged, Blinking Blue = Pairing Mode.
