Our Philosophy

Form follows acoustic emotion.

FoldBeat was founded on a simple realization: Most portable speakers look like disposable plastic toys that clash with thoughtful living spaces. We set out to design a speaker that sits naturally alongside marble, ceramics, art books, and sunlight.

“We do not design speakers to hide away in a drawer. We design them to be touched, unfolded, and displayed as everyday art.”
— FoldBeat Design Studio, Mumbai
01 / The Hinge

Mechanical Integrity

The heart of FoldBeat is its gold-anodized dual-axis hinge. Tested through 20,000 cycles, it allows the panels to rest securely at any angle—whether angled on your desk, spread flat across a table, or folded shut for transit.

02 / The Acoustics

Resonance Without Rattle

Unlike mass-market speakers that distort at volume, our dual Helmholtz chambers maintain clean acoustic pressure. Bass notes stay punchy, while acoustic guitars and vocals retain their natural warmth.

03 / The Materials

Tactile Longevity

We combined satin-finish composite panels with brushed gold metal accents. Every surface is finished to resist fingerprints and wear, ensuring it looks as beautiful five years from now as it does today.

Tactile Precision

Buttons you can feel. Engineering you can trust.

In an era dominated by temperamental touch-screens and capacitive sensors, FoldBeat deliberately retains physical, satisfying mechanical buttons. Click to pause, press to raise volume, and feel the solid dual-knuckle gold hinge adjust smoothly with your fingertips.
